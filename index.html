Our Story

Two friends, one way of seeing.

Hollis & Vale began with a borrowed camera and a cousin's wedding in Bath. Fourteen years later, we're a small studio of four — still shooting every wedding ourselves, still printing every album by hand.

We believe photographs should feel like memory, not marketing: honest colour, soft contrast, and room to breathe. We work quietly, guide gently when it helps, and otherwise disappear into your day so you can live it.

Eleanor HollisLead Photographer

    The Experience

    From first hello to heirloom

    A calm, considered process — so the only thing you have to think about on the day is each other.

    1. I

      Conversation

      A call (or a coffee) to hear your story, your plans and what matters most to you.

    2. II

      Planning

      Timelines, light, locations and family lists — handled together, weeks in advance.

    3. III

      The Day

      We arrive early, move quietly and gently guide only when it helps the moment.

    4. IV

      Heirloom

      A curated gallery in four weeks; a linen-bound, hand-printed album to follow.
